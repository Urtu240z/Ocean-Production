## Scope and authority

The diagnostic shader imports the authoritative texture evaluation, Coastal composition and guarded inverse functions verbatim through the existing `ocean_envelope_shader_source.gd` compiler. It adds fine Jacobian/conditioning output and FP64 root output. The material-coordinate closure also preserves the material seed in FP64 and checks local continuation with projected steps no larger than 1 mm. It introduces no alternate ocean transform or production query mode.

FFT construction, LONG/MID/SHORT composition, Coastal, visual clipmap, BreakerCarrier, the production query modes, asynchronous readback, forces and Jolt are frozen. No new runtime atlas, atomic scatter, support filter, force law, practical 16-tile benchmark or RTX hardware target is part of this audit.

The renderer is native Godot 4.7.1 Forward+/D3D12 on the GTX 970. The scene uses the same frozen FFT+Coastal fixture as the earlier GPU proof, with breaker/foam effects disabled in the validation instance. It makes no new Carrier-contract claim. Headless runs are syntax checks only; they are not GPU proofs. The existing standard-gzip archives are reused in place and hashed, not duplicated.

Adaptive uncertainty intervals describe sampled cell/identity uncertainty; they do not mathematically exclude undiscovered roots or subcell topology. Area-weighted means and medians use clipped cell area. All six hypothetical support thresholds (1%, 5%, 10%, 25%, 50%, 75%) remain validation counterfactuals.

Its lateral spacing is 0.90 m at the front and 1.00 m at the rear; longitudinal separation is 2.20 m. The legacy water-physics system requires these four markers.

The current 4-contact GPU proof layout uses those same XZ positions. Its 8- and 16-contact proof layouts use two rails at X=±0.48 m, spanning Z=-1.15 to +1.05 m. Longitudinal spacing is approximately 0.7333 m and 0.3143 m respectively. These are proof layouts, not a tuned new hull-force implementation. The legacy hull-pressure effect defaults to half extents `(0.6,1.45)` m; with the actual markers it derives approximately `(0.475,1.10)` m. That interaction footprint is not an established integration/support radius for each buoyancy point.

The four-marker planform is a trapezoid of approximately 2.09 m². An even arithmetic allocation would be 0.5225 m²/contact. The proof rails span a 2.112 m² rectangle, giving 0.264 m²/contact at eight points or 0.132 m²/contact at sixteen. These are contextual area allocations, not implemented force weights or contact footprints. Features of 2 mm, 1 cm, 5 cm and even 20 cm are smaller than the closest proof-point spacing of 31.4 cm. Their being smaller than point spacing does not establish that an individual sampled contact should ignore them. The 80 cm diagnostic disc has area about 0.503 m², comparable to the four-point arithmetic allocation, while the smaller discs probe much more local support.

## Architectural result and acceptance

**Choose OUTCOME A — highest-Y contract stays. Acceptance is PARTIAL.** Cases 0 and 1 have roughly 0.215–0.217 m² measured connected support and at least 46.76% and 51.13% coverage of a 20 cm anchor disc at every sampled tick in the full window. Case 2 also meets the specified broad/persistent indicator and retains at least 45.86% support of a 10 cm anchor disc throughout the window. Their atlas misses reach 0.785 m, 0.782 m and 0.101 m. These are physically significant missed upper sheets under the phase's review indicators.

Outcome B is unsupported: broad sheets are demonstrably missed, and the six-case evidence does not establish a predominantly tiny, short-lived population. Outcome C requires an established second population of narrow mathematical slivers as well as meaningful broad folds. The uncertain cases could eventually motivate that revision, but their small known masks, finite-difference conditioning and fixed-target rank losses do not prove narrow physical support or short sheet lifetimes.

PARTIAL follows the requested acceptance definition: cases 3–5 remain poorly bounded in winning support and time, so the architectural recommendation is provisional rather than PASS. This is a completed capped collection with zero audit errors. It is not an analytic topology/completeness certificate. No finite-support threshold or new force law is selected.

Recommend **PHYS-GPU-ENVELOPE-ADAPTIVE-1**, adaptive fold tessellation / upper-envelope construction, as the next validation proposal. It has not been started. The unresolved physical interpretation must stay visible when evaluating that proposal; highest-Y remains the frozen production contract in this delivery.
